# LinkedIn job-application policy

Logged-in LinkedIn interaction is manual-only for this extension.

- Do not use `browser-fast`, `browser`, Selenium, scripts, or extensions to click, type, submit, connect, message, apply, or otherwise mutate the logged-in LinkedIn site.
- Browser-assisted research may use public information outside the authenticated session when appropriate.
- For jobs discovered through LinkedIn, prefer resolving the role to the employer's official careers page or external ATS and continue there.
- Prepare factual answers, fit analysis, and application text for the operator, but leave LinkedIn account interaction to the operator.

This policy is stronger than site/platform strategy memory and must not be overridden by learned job-application memory.
